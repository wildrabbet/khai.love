No forms, no tracking, no analytics on this site. The page you are reading is flat HTML.

What to include

  1. What should be true afterwards? The thing that is not true now and needs to be. This is the single most useful sentence you can write.
  2. How would we know it worked? A number, a behaviour, a test you would run. Work without a check is not finished, it is just stopped.
  3. What already exists? Code, data, a half-built attempt, a hard constraint. Especially anything that has already been tried and did not work.
  4. What would make this urgent or not? Deadlines change the plan legitimately. Guessing at them does not.
