Inside the studio / Ongoing projects

Ideas with
some depth.

A selection of the systems and creative projects behind KHAI. Games, tools and the foundations that bring them together.

01

Academy

Character-driven card-game opponents, with individual decks, play personalities and combo knowledge. The work combines board evaluation, character systems and a benchmark lab.

02

Ripper

A game-asset extraction and conversion toolkit. Reads archive and model formats and produces standard assets including glTF, GLB and PNG.

03

Rabbit’s Realm

An interactive browser experience built from converted assets and rendered in real time. A proving ground for the asset conversion work.

04

The Platform

The studio’s coordination layer: a shared decision record, defined responsibilities and planning spaces that keep work connected.

05

Conversion Pipeline

Model, animation and lighting conversion into open formats, with attention to skeletons, animation retargeting and colour accuracy.
