Services

Practical digital services for businesses.

From a new website to a custom internal system, we focus on work that makes your business easier to find, easier to deal with or easier to run.

Websites

Business websites that customers can actually use.

A good website should quickly answer who you are, what you offer, where you operate and what a customer should do next. We design around those basics first, then make the presentation feel polished and distinctive.

  • New business websites
  • Restaurant, café and hospitality sites
  • Trades and professional service websites
  • Mobile-first layouts
  • Menus, galleries, service pages and FAQs
  • Booking, enquiry and quote links
  • Domain and deployment support
Good fit: your current site looks dated, is hard to use on a phone, does not explain the business clearly or you simply do not have one yet.
Online business

Ordering, sales and customer actions.

We can make it easier for customers to take the next step online without forcing your business into a system that does not suit the way you work.

  • Product and service catalogues
  • Online store setup and customisation
  • Ordering and payment integrations
  • Booking links and reservation flows
  • Quote and enquiry forms
  • Lead capture and customer follow-up
  • Third-party service integrations
Good fit: customers currently have to call, message or hunt around to complete a simple action that could be handled cleanly online.
Automation

Reduce repetitive admin and manual handoffs.

If the same information is being copied between emails, forms, spreadsheets or systems, there is often a better way. We map the process first, then automate only the parts that are safe and useful to automate.

  • Form and enquiry routing
  • Email and notification workflows
  • Spreadsheet and data automation
  • Recurring report generation
  • Customer and internal task workflows
  • Agent-assisted business processes
  • Approval and review steps where needed
Good fit: staff spend time repeating predictable digital tasks or important follow-up depends too heavily on someone remembering every step.
Custom software

Software built around your operation.

Sometimes a business has outgrown spreadsheets and generic platforms but does not need an enormous enterprise project. We can build focused software around the actual workflow.

  • Internal dashboards
  • Booking and scheduling tools
  • Customer or staff portals
  • Operational tools
  • Data and reporting systems
  • API and service integrations
  • Custom web applications
Good fit: off-the-shelf tools force awkward workarounds, duplicate data or create more administration than they save.
Repairs & improvements

Fix what you already have.

You may not need a rebuild. We can inspect an existing site or software project, identify the highest-impact problems and improve it in sensible stages.

  • Website refreshes
  • Mobile and layout fixes
  • Broken forms and integrations
  • Performance improvements
  • Bug investigation
  • Codebase cleanup
  • Technical rescue and modernisation
Good fit: something important already exists, but it is unreliable, frustrating to change or no longer reflects the quality of the business.
Ongoing support

Reliable technical help after launch.

Digital work rarely ends forever on launch day. We can continue helping with content changes, improvements, fixes and new ideas without turning every request into a brand-new project.

  • Website updates
  • New landing and service pages
  • Ongoing technical fixes
  • Small feature additions
  • Automation improvements
  • Monitoring and maintenance planning
  • Technical advice as the business changes
Good fit: you want a consistent technical partner rather than finding someone new each time a small but important change comes up.
Pricing approach

Scope first, then a clear price.

We do not hide a simple job behind vague consulting language. Small, well-defined work can be quoted directly. Larger or uncertain projects are broken into clear stages so you can decide how far to proceed.

Fixed-scope work

Best for websites, defined improvements, small integrations and clearly specified deliverables.

Staged projects

Best for larger builds where discovery, design, implementation and rollout should be agreed separately.

Ongoing support

Best when your business expects regular updates, maintenance or incremental improvements.
